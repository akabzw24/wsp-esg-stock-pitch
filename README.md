# WSP ESG Investment Thesis – CFAC Portfolio Management Competition

This project presents an in-depth equity research pitch developed for the **2025 CFAC Portfolio Management Competition**, where our team proposed **WSP Global (TSE: WSP)** as a high-conviction long-term buy.

Our thesis centered on WSP’s leadership in **sustainable infrastructure and digital engineering**, supported by ESG tailwinds, strong free cash flow growth, and defensible market position in smart cities and green transition advisory.

---

## Thesis Highlights

- **Ticker:** WSP.TO  
- **Sector:** Industrials / Professional Services  
- **Market Cap:** ~$24B CAD  
- **Rating:** Long-Term Buy  
- **Time Horizon:** 3–5 Years

---

## Key Thesis 

- **ESG Leadership:** WSP ranks among the top engineering firms globally for ESG alignment, making it an attractive pick for institutional ESG flows.
- **Recurring Revenue:** High portion of contracted revenues in infrastructure lifecycle management.
- **M&A Strategy:** Aggressive but smart inorganic growth in Europe and North America, particularly in climate/energy consulting.
- **Tech Differentiation:** WSP integrates AI, GIS, and digital twins into project delivery.

---

## Financial Valuation

- **DCF Valuation**  
  - 3-scenario model: **Base, Bull, Bear**  
  - 5-year forecast of Free Cash Flow (FCF)  
  - Terminal Value using exit multiple method  
  - Unlevered IRRs:  
    - Base: ~16%  
    - Bull: ~23%  
    - Bear: ~11%

- **Relative Valuation**  
  - Compared against peers like Jacobs, AECOM, SNC-Lavalin  
  - Multiples: EV/EBITDA, P/E, EV/FCF
